Snooker Clip Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snooker Clip, developed by Edge Pair Labs, handles information when you use the application.

1. 100% Local Processing

Snooker Clip is designed to process video locally on your devices. Video processing and storage performed by Snooker Clip are carried out locally on the device.

Snooker Clip does not require cloud storage or a cloud service to perform its core video capture and snooker shot analysis functions.

We do not upload your recorded snooker videos or analyzed video frames to Edge Pair Labs servers for cloud processing.

2. Camera Access

Snooker Clip may require access to the device camera to provide video capture and snooker analysis functionality.

Camera access is used to capture video for the features selected by the user. Video frames used for analysis are processed locally on the device.

3. Local Network Video Streaming

Snooker Clip can use one device as a camera and send a video stream over a local network to another device running Snooker Clip. The receiving device can capture and analyze the snooker shots in real time.

This communication is intended to occur between the user's own devices on their local network. Snooker Clip does not require the video stream to be uploaded to Edge Pair Labs or stored in cloud storage.

4. Internet and Network Access

Snooker Clip may use network access for communication between devices and for network-camera functionality.

The app's core video processing and snooker analysis are performed locally. We do not use your video content for remote cloud processing.

5. Personal Information

Snooker Clip does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, address, telephone number, or email address in order to use its core functionality.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Information Sharing

Edge Pair Labs does not sell or share your recorded videos, camera frames, or snooker analysis data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Video streamed between your devices is transmitted for the functionality selected by you and is not intentionally transmitted to Edge Pair Labs servers.

7. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Video recordings, clips, and other files created by Snooker Clip may be stored locally on your device according to the features you use.

Because this information is stored locally on your device, you can delete locally stored recordings and clips using the app's available controls or by deleting the corresponding files from your device.

Edge Pair Labs does not maintain a cloud database containing your Snooker Clip video recordings or locally processed video data.

8. Security

Snooker Clip is designed to process video locally and to avoid transmitting video to Edge Pair Labs cloud servers.

When using local network streaming, users should use a trusted network and should take appropriate steps to protect their devices and network.

9. Children's Privacy

Snooker Clip is a general-purpose sports application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
