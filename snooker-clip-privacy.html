Snooker Clip Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snooker Clip, developed by Edge Pair Labs, handles information when you use the application.

1. Local Processing

Snooker Clip is designed to perform video processing locally on your devices.

Video processing and storage performed by Snooker Clip are carried out locally on the user's device. Snooker Clip does not require cloud storage or a cloud service for its core video capture and snooker analysis features.

Snooker Clip does not operate a server for uploading, storing, or remotely processing your videos or camera frames.

2. Camera Access

Snooker Clip may require access to the device camera to provide video capture and snooker analysis functionality.

Camera access is used to capture video for features selected by the user. Video frames used for snooker analysis are processed locally on the device.

3. Local Network Video Streaming

Snooker Clip can use one device as a camera and send a video stream over a local network to another device running Snooker Clip. The receiving device can capture and analyze snooker shots in real time.

This communication is intended to occur between the user's own devices on their local network.

The video stream is not uploaded to Edge Pair Labs servers or stored in Edge Pair Labs cloud storage.

4. Internet and Network Access

Snooker Clip may use network access for communication between devices and for network-camera functionality.

Snooker Clip does not operate a cloud service for processing or storing user video.

An Internet connection is not required for the app's local video processing and analysis functionality when the required devices can communicate through a suitable local network.

5. Personal Information

Snooker Clip does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, address, telephone number, or email address in order to use its core functionality.

Snooker Clip does not operate a server or cloud service for collecting or processing your app data.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Crash Logs

When an error occurs, Snooker Clip may create a crash log and store it locally on your device.

Crash logs are not automatically transmitted to Edge Pair Labs or to any third party.

Snooker Clip may provide an option for you to manually send a crash log to Edge Pair Labs by email. If you choose to do so, the crash log will be attached to an email sent by you to:

snooker.clip.com@gmail.com

Sending a crash log is optional and requires an action by the user.

A crash log may contain technical information about the application, device, operating system, and the error that occurred. The information contained in a crash log is used to investigate and resolve technical problems with Snooker Clip.

7. Information Sharing

Edge Pair Labs does not sell or share your recorded videos, camera frames, or locally processed snooker analysis data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Video streamed between your devices is transmitted for the functionality selected by you and is not intentionally transmitted to Edge Pair Labs servers.

If you voluntarily send a crash log to Edge Pair Labs by email, the information in that email and its attachments will be received by Edge Pair Labs for the purpose of providing technical support and improving the application.

8. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Video recordings, clips, crash logs, and other files created by Snooker Clip may be stored locally on your device according to the features you use.

Because this information is stored locally on your device, you can delete locally stored recordings, clips, and crash logs using the app's available controls or by deleting the corresponding files from your device.

Edge Pair Labs does not maintain a cloud database containing your Snooker Clip video recordings, camera frames, or locally generated crash logs.

If you voluntarily email a crash log to Edge Pair Labs, the email and its attachment may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the reported problem and provide technical support.

9. Security

Snooker Clip is designed to process video locally and does not automatically transmit video or crash logs to Edge Pair Labs servers.

When using local network streaming, users should use a trusted network and take appropriate steps to protect their devices and network.

10. Children's Privacy

Snooker Clip is a general-purpose sports application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
